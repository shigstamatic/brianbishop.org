Album Era Support

Album Era is an album-first music library for Apple Music. This page covers basic support information for users and App Store review.

Help

Getting Started

Album Era requires Apple Music access and an active Apple Music subscription for catalog search and playback. Use Search to add complete albums, Organize Shelves to curate your collection, and Album Discovery to find suggestions from your Apple Music Library.

Data

Backups and Sync

Album Era can sync your collection through your private iCloud account and automatically keeps local backups that can be restored from inside the app.
