Album Era

Make full-album listening intentional again.

Build a home for the albums you love, rediscover forgotten favorites, and make full-album listening feel intentional again.

Album Era is an independent, album-first companion for Apple Music. The app is free. Playback and catalog search require an active Apple Music subscription, purchased separately from Apple.

Download on the App Store

Your collection

Albums deserve a place of their own.

Collect the classics you return to, discoveries you do not want to lose, soundtracks to particular seasons of your life, and albums you have always meant to hear all the way through. Add complete albums from Apple Music and build a collection that can grow from a few favorites to thousands of albums.

01

Shape your shelves

Organize albums onto custom shelves for genres, moods, decades, artists, scenes, memories, projects, or anything else that makes sense to you.

02

Let Smart Shelves help

Create dynamic shelves from rules such as artist, genre, release decade, and listening history, while keeping your own labels and organization intact.

03

Pick up where you stopped

Saved album positions make it easy to return to a long album without losing your place.

04

Rediscover and explore

Album Discovery brings back albums you have not heard recently and offers suggestions inspired by your Apple Music Library.

05

Bring albums into the car

Browse shelves and albums and control playback through Album Era’s focused CarPlay experience.

06

Keep your collection safe

Optional iCloud sync keeps supported collection data available across your devices, while automatic local backups give you a recovery history inside the app.
