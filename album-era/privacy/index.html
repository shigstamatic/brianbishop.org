Album Era Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Summary

Your Album Era Data

Album Era stores your album collection, shelves, shelf labels, search history, playback state, listening stats, and discovery preferences on your device. If iCloud Sync is enabled, that data syncs through your private iCloud account.

Access

Developer Access

Album Era does not operate a separate developer server for your collection data, does not use advertising tracking, and does not include third-party analytics. Your private iCloud data is handled by Apple and is not visible in the developer portal.

Apple

Apple Music and iCloud

Album Era uses Apple Music for catalog search and playback, and uses iCloud for optional sync. Apple handles Apple Music account, subscription, catalog, playback, and iCloud service data under Apple's privacy policies.

Controls

Your Controls

You can clear resume states, listening stats, search history, sort preferences, shelf labels, discovery suggestions, local backups, or your full Album Era library from Settings. You can also export and restore local backups from inside the app.
